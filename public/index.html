How to play

Place one orb per turn into an empty cell or a cell you already own — never into an opponent's.

A cell's critical mass is its number of orthogonal neighbours: 2 in a corner, 3 on an edge, 4 inside. Reach it and the cell explodes, sending one orb into each neighbour.

Every cell that receives an orb turns your colour, orbs and all. If that tips it to its own critical mass it explodes too, and the chain runs until the board settles.

Once everyone has had a turn, any player left with no orbs is out. Last one standing wins.
