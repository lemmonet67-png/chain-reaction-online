Rules

Place one orb per turn into an empty cell or a cell you already own. Never into an opponent's.

A cell's critical mass is its number of orthogonal neighbours — 2 in a corner, 3 on an edge, 4 inside. Reach it and the cell detonates, sending one orb to each neighbour.

Every cell that receives an orb flips to your colour, orbs and all. If it hits its own critical mass it detonates too, and the chain runs until the board settles.

Once everyone has had a turn, any reactor left with zero orbs is out. Last one standing wins.
